ModelQuest — AI Model Evolution Dashboard

Interactive Power BI Dashboard

🔍 Business Need

AI teams, researchers, and product leads needed a single view of how model development has evolved over time — not just in size, but in training efficiency and performance-to-cost ratio. This dashboard tracks 15+ years of AI model launches to surface hidden trends in compute usage, breakthrough years, and parameter-per-FLOP gains.

📌 Key Insights

  • Launch heat‑map — exposes the 2023 model sprint (+120% YoY volume).
  • Training‑hour vs. performance bubble plot — reveals over-performers vs. compute-heavy laggards.
  • Cumulative model count card — shows exponential acceleration across model families.

🧱 Tech Stack

Python · pandas · Power Query · DAX · Power BI

📂 Dataset Info

Curated from public leaderboards and GitHub model metadata · 200+ AI models · Tracked over 15 years · Normalized by training hours, parameters, and compute efficiency

💻 My Role

I sourced and normalized raw model metadata, engineered a semantic Power BI model with DAX for training efficiency (parameters-per-FLOP), and designed an interactive timeline and :hover visualizations that let users drill into any model family in seconds.

This project demonstrates my data-wrangling agility and ability to craft insight-rich visual storytelling — skills I’d bring to any role where raw metrics need to become strategy-shaping insight.
