ModelQuest — AI Model Evolution Dashboard

Interactive dashboard

Why it matters: This Power BI dashboard tracks 15 years of AI‑model releases, mapping training hours, parameter counts, and launch cadence to reveal a 5× jump in compute‑efficiency from 2014‑2024. Product leads use the timeline to benchmark R&D velocity and spot breakthrough years.

My role: Sourced model‑metadata CSVs from public leaderboards, normalised them in Python (pandas) and built a semantic model in Power BI with DAX measures for parameter‑per‑FLOP efficiency. Designed a clean, interactive timeline that surfaces :hover tooltips and drill‑through views for any model family.

Key insights at a glance

  • Launch heat‑map – shows the sprint of releases in 2023 (⟶ +120 % YoY).
  • Training‑hour vs. performance bubble plot – outliers flag models punching above their compute budget.
  • Cumulative model count card – visual proof of the field’s exponential pace.

Tech stack

Python • pandas • Power Query • DAX • Power BI

This project demonstrates data‑wrangling agility and visual storytelling—skills I’d bring to any role that needs to turn raw metrics into strategy‑shaping insight.
