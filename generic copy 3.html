Global Cyber‑Incident Insights Dashboard

Interactive Power BI Dashboard

🔍 Business Need

Cybersecurity executives and SecOps teams needed real-time insight into breach trends, loss exposure, and exploit activity. This dashboard transforms global breach data into continuous intelligence to support faster triage, incident prioritization, and ROI tracking on cyber defense investments.

📌 Key Wins

  • –138 M $ saved in potential exposure by isolating top 5 zero-day exploit patterns.
  • 60 % faster incident triage through risk heatmap and regional drill-through.
  • 24/7 refresh via automated pipeline; monitored under a 99.8 % SLA.

🧱 Tech Stack

Apache Airflow · Python (pandas, pyarrow) · AWS S3 · Snowflake · Power BI (Fabric) · DAX

⚙️ Architecture Overview

  • Ingest: Airflow DAGs extract raw CSVs from Kaggle, validate schema, and store them in S3.
  • Transform & Load: Python scripts clean data → partitioned and staged in Snowflake by year and country.
  • Delivery: Power BI semantic model with incremental refresh and RLS by region.
