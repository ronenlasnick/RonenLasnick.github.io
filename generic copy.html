Plant Co. Sales Performance Dashboard

Interactive Power BI Dashboard

🔍 Business Need

Plant Co. needed actionable visibility into sales performance across products and regions, especially to uncover profit gaps, seasonal trends, and account-level underperformance. Decision-makers were using disconnected Excel files—this project centralized the intelligence and made it interactive.

📌 Key Wins

  • Highlighted negative −512K ₪ YTD vs. PYTD sales drop and isolated the largest contributors: China, Sweden, and France.
  • Used dynamic waterfall and scatter visuals to analyze regional swings and profit margin segments.
  • Introduced GP% segmentation dashboard to help sales leads focus on strategic, high-margin product groups.
  • Dashboard adopted in monthly board reviews and weekly cross-regional sales calls—driving 7 % GP recovery in Q4.

🧱 Tech Stack

Excel CSV exports ➝ Power Query (cleaning, reshaping) ➝ DAX (time intelligence + KPI logic) ➝ Power BI Pro Service for delivery.

📂 Dataset Info

2.4M sales records • Monthly granularity • Multi-country data • Product-level margin, GP%, and trend indicators
