Plant Co. Sales Performance Dashboard

Why it matters: This end‑to‑end Power BI solution turns 2.4 million sales rows into a single pane‑of‑glass for executives. Dynamic KPIs compare YTD vs. PYTD trends, a monthly waterfall pinpoints profit swings, and segmentation by GP % × volume surfaces low‑margin outliers.

Business impact: The dashboard revealed a −138 K ₪ gross‑profit gap concentrated in Poland & China, prompting a pricing realignment that improved quarterly GP +7 %. Stakeholders now track performance in real time instead of waiting on end‑of‑month Excel dumps.

My contribution: Data modelling in Power Query, DAX measures for YTD vs. PYTD, custom waterfall chart, and UX polish for exec‑level clarity. Result: a shareable link powering cross‑country stand‑ups and budget reviews.

Tech stack: Excel CSV ➝ Power Query ➝ DAX ➝ Power BI Service (publish & share).
