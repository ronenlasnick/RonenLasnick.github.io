Plant Co. Sales Performance Dashboard

Interactive Power BI Dashboard

🔍 Business Need

Executives and regional managers at Plant Co. needed a single source of truth for tracking gross profit, performance by region, and product group margins. Instead of relying on end-of-month Excel reports, they wanted real-time access to sales fluctuations, underperforming segments, and YTD vs. PYTD comparisons.

📌 Key Wins

  • Pinpointed −138 K ₪ gross profit gap in Poland & China → pricing strategy realignment.
  • Custom monthly waterfall visualized regional fluctuations in profit contribution.
  • 7 % quarterly GP increase after rollout; now used in recurring cross-country stand-ups.

🧱 Tech Stack

Excel CSV ➝ Power Query for transformation ➝ DAX for KPIs and measures ➝ Power BI Service for publishing and sharing.

📂 Dataset Info

2.4 million sales rows • Country and product-level granularity • Monthly and quarterly comparisons
