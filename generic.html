E-commerce Behavior Analysis

This Project Is Comprehensive Insights for E-Commerce, explores user behavior patterns within a multi-category online retail store. The dataset, sourced from Kaggle’s REES46, covers 57 GB of big data spanning several months of transactional activity. Key metrics such as total views, product carts, pricing trends, and product counts were analyzed to uncover valuable insights into customer interactions. Through advanced filtering, performance tracking, and visual storytelling techniques, the project provides actionable insights for enhancing sales strategies and improving user engagement.

The Power BI dashboard, embedded on this page, showcases an intuitive interface designed for dynamic data exploration. With brand-wise product analysis, monthly sales performance trends, and top product highlights, this project demonstrates the potential of big data and business intelligence in shaping e-commerce success. Dive in to explore the interactive visuals that offer a deeper understanding of user engagement and market behavior trends.
