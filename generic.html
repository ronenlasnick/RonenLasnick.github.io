E‑commerce Behaviour Dashboard

Interactive Power BI Dashboard

*This dashboard is over 8.8 GB and may take up to 5 minutes to load depending on your internet speed and Power BI traffic.

🔍 Business Need

Retailers often struggle to identify patterns in user navigation, cart abandonment, and peak demand. This dashboard transforms over 135M behavioral data rows into actionable insights, empowering leaders to optimize merchandising, promotions, and product availability in real time.

📌 Key Wins

  • Flagged understocked SKUs linked to −7 % weekly lost revenue → sent to auto-replenishment flow.
  • Highlighted high‑margin items with ≥15 % conversion boost potential → guided targeted campaigns.
  • Built advanced brand/category filters → eliminated ~6 hrs/week of repetitive ad hoc SQL analysis.

🧱 Tech Stack

Python (pandas · pyarrow) for ETL → Power Query for modeling → Power BI for visualization and interactivity.

📂 Dataset Info

REES46 e‑commerce behavior dataset (Kaggle) · 135M rows · 14 columns · Oct 2019 – Apr 2020 · Raw size: 57 GB · Power BI file: 8.8 GB
