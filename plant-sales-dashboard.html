Plant Co. Sales Performance

A simulated business report for sales, gross profit, and geographic performance, labeled clearly so the scope is not confused with a client production outcome.

Interactive Power BI Report

Problem

Sales leaders need a fast way to compare current performance against prior periods by region and product.

Build

Modeled sales and gross-profit measures with DAX time intelligence and a report layout built for scanning.

Delivery

Used waterfall, scatter, and matrix-style views to separate total movement from country and product detail.

Technical Focus

  • YTD and prior-year comparisons for sales and gross profit.
  • Published report structure: compact single-page executive view with eight focused visuals.
  • Segmentation by geography, product group, and profitability.
  • Executive reporting layout with drill-down paths for deeper analysis.
